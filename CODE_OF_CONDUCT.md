# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their background or level of experience.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient. Assume that the other person means well.
- Talk about the code and the idea, not about the person.
- Accept that people disagree. Give reasons, and listen to theirs.
- Help newcomers. A question that looks simple is still a good question.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, and personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- The publication of private data of another person.

## Where it applies

In issues, pull requests, discussions, and all other project spaces.

## Report a problem

Contact the maintainers in private. Use the "Report content" menu on the comment or issue, which goes to GitHub, or send a private report through the [security advisory form](https://github.com/dansku/sshwave/security/advisories/new) and say that it is a conduct report. The maintainers keep your report confidential. They can edit or remove content, and they can block a person who does not follow these rules.
